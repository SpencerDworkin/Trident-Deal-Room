New Jersey sheriff’s-sale acquisition

At a sheriff’s sale the opening bid is the debt, not the house. We buy where the two are furthest apart.

Trident acquires one-to-four-unit homes at New Jersey sheriff’s sales and holds them under Housing Choice Voucher tenancy. It is raising a first fund from family offices.

Every residential filing on the covered calendars, by recorded debt as a share of county median value

417 filings with a published judgment across Bergen, Burlington, Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex and Monmouth at the current pull. The foreclosing lender opens the bidding at its judgment. Left of 0.4 the judgment is under forty cents on the dollar of the county median; the screen refuses everything from 0.8 up. Median: 0.65.

Strategy
Acquire 1–4 unit residential property at New Jersey sheriff’s sales, screened on recorded debt against county median value. Hold under voucher tenancy for income.

Who We Are

Trident is a residential real estate fund with an AI sourcing engine. Our models read every county sheriff’s calendar in New Jersey each day, pull the docket, the judgment and the assessor record behind every filing, and rank the homes where the lender’s opening bid sits furthest below what the house is worth. Our principals underwrite the shortlist, attend the sale, and hold what we buy under Housing Choice Voucher tenancy. We are a Patel Legacy Properties company.

What We Do

Source. Underwrite. Hold.

The AI does the reading. The principals do the buying. The fund does the holding.

01 · Source

AI reads every filing

Every scheduled sale in six counties is pulled daily. The models match each filing to its docket, judgment, plaintiff and assessor value, score it on recorded debt against county median, and refuse the files where the discount is an illusion.

How it works 02 · Underwrite

People buy, to a written ceiling

Title, liens, occupancy and valuation are worked on a dated checklist for every shortlisted home. A principal sets the ceiling from the underwriting, attends the sale in person, and stops at the number.

Strategy 03 · Hold

Voucher tenancy, for income

Each home is brought to the housing authority’s inspection standard and leased to a Housing Choice Voucher holder. The authority pays the larger part of the rent directly, every month. Investors see the same book we run on.

For investors

Why Work with Us

Deal flow is the edge.

Nobody else reads the whole calendar. Hundreds of sales are scheduled every month across our counties. A person can open a few dozen files. Our models read all of them, every day, and hand the principals a ranked shortlist with the reasons attached.

The price is set by a court record, not a seller. At a sheriff’s sale the lender opens the bidding at its judgment. The AI’s job is to find the filings where that number sits far below the house; our job is to confirm it and buy.

Cash, discipline, and a person in the room. No lender at the table, a ceiling written down before the sale, and a principal standing at every auction. The model proposes; it never bids.

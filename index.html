Who We Are

Trident acquires one-to-four-unit homes at New Jersey sheriff’s sales, where the opening bid is the lender’s judgment rather than the house’s value, and holds them under Housing Choice Voucher tenancy. Our expertise encompasses foreclosure-calendar screening, title and lien underwriting, and voucher-tenancy management across six New Jersey counties. We are a Patel Legacy Properties company.

What We Do

Screen. Buy. Hold.

Three jobs, in that order, done the same way for every property.

01 · Screen

Every filing, the same rules

Each scheduled sale in six counties is pulled daily, matched against a nine-rule buy box and scored on recorded debt against county median value.

How we buy 02 · Buy

For cash, to a written ceiling

Title, valuation, occupancy and liens are worked on a dated checklist before the sale. A principal attends, bids to a ceiling set from the underwriting, and stops there.

What we do 03 · Hold

Voucher tenancy, for income

Each home is brought to the housing authority’s inspection standard and leased to a Housing Choice Voucher holder. The authority pays the larger part of the rent directly, every month.

Why Work with Us

Discipline is the product.

The screen is written down. Nine rules with numbers in them, run on every filing in every covered county in the same order. Neither principal can override a verdict for a property they like.

Cash, not debt. A sheriff’s sale takes twenty percent at the gavel and the balance in thirty days, in certified funds. There is no lender at the table and no lender ahead of investors.

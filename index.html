Why Trident

Four things we can say in a sentence each.

Deal Flow

We see the whole market

Our platform reads public records across our markets every day and ranks every opportunity. We are not waiting for a broker to call. Our deal flow is proprietary and it is continuous.

Basis

We buy below value

The homes we acquire are priced by a court supervised process, not by a willing seller. The margin is captured on the day we buy. Our returns do not depend on a rising market.

Income

We hold without leverage

Unlevered ownership and rent paid largely by housing authorities on a published schedule. No refinancing risk, no lender covenants, no forced sales in a downturn.

Alignment

We invest alongside you

The principals commit their own capital on the same terms. Every investor has direct access to both principals and property level reporting every quarter.

Why Now

The field is clearing.

Foreclosure volume in the United States has risen for seven consecutive quarters. The Northeast has the deepest housing shortage in the country and builds the least. And from January 2027, federal law bars the largest institutional investors from buying one and two unit homes, including at foreclosure. Disciplined private capital, operating below that threshold, has rarely had less competition for the assets we pursue.

The Opportunity

Strategy

Source. Acquire. Operate.

Source. The platform identifies residential assets of one to four units where the price on offer sits well below value, and ranks them for underwriting.

Acquire. A principal underwrites each opportunity, sets a ceiling in advance and buys for cash. When the number is wrong, we pass.

Operate. Homes are brought to inspection standard and leased for the long term under tenancies supported by public housing programs. Distributions come from rent.

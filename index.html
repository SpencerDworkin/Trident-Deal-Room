The Deal Desk

Every sheriff sale in Western New York, already underwritten

Six counties publish foreclosure notices on their own schedules, with no valuation, no condition read, and no financing context. Our desk ingests all of it — sheriff sales, tax foreclosures, REO, probate, and pre-foreclosure — and returns a basis, three exit models, and a maximum allowable offer before the sale date.

SOURCE

Six counties

Monroe, Ontario, Wayne, Genesee, Livingston, and Orleans dockets normalized into one record format.

DILIGENCE

Value & scope

After-repair value from paired comps, plus a condition band and a rehab budget on every parcel.

UNDERWRITE

Three exits

Flip, buy-and-hold, and BRRRR modeled side by side with DSCR, cash-on-cash, and yield on cost.

EXECUTE

Bid to deed

Bid ladder, walk-away number, deposit mechanics, and the handoff through the referee's deed.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Real Estate Excellence

Founded over thirty years ago, LLD Enterprises is a Rochester, New York-based family-owned real estate investment and development firm. We combine family capital with deep expertise in acquiring, developing, and managing high-quality commercial real estate assets.

Our portfolio encompasses over 2 million square feet of retail, office, and industrial properties, plus 240 acres of development land, serving over 600 tenants across the northeast, southeast, and midwest.

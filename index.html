Who We Are

Operators, with our own capital in every deal.

Trident is a Patel Legacy Properties company. We find commercial property that is priced below what it is worth, buy it with our own money alongside a few investors, and run it for income. Our technology finds the deals. Our experience decides which ones to buy.

What We Buy

Mixed use and retail, close to home.

Mixed Use

Storefronts with apartments above

Buildings on established main streets where a shop pays the ground floor and homes pay the rest. Two kinds of income under one roof.

Retail

Neighborhood and single tenant retail

Small centers and single tenant buildings with tenants people visit every week. Bought at a price that works on the rent in place.

Where

New Jersey and nearby

Our home market first, and New York, Pennsylvania and Connecticut as the right properties appear. We buy where we can drive to the building.

How We Work

Find it. Underwrite it. Buy it well.

We see more than a listing feed. Our own software reads public records and the market across our area every day and flags the property that is priced below its worth, often before it is widely marketed.

A principal underwrites every deal. We walk the building, read the leases, set the price we will pay and do not go above it.

We invest alongside our partners. Our own capital goes into every deal on the same terms. We manage the property ourselves. Distributions come from income.

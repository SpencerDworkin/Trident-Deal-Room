What We Do

Source. Acquire. Operate.

01

Source

Our platform reads public records across our markets every day and identifies residential assets where the price on offer sits well below the value of the home. Most of the market never sees these opportunities. We see all of them.

02

Acquire

A principal underwrites every opportunity and sets a ceiling in advance. We buy for cash, at a structural discount, and we walk away when the number is wrong. Discipline is the strategy.

03

Operate

Homes are stabilized and leased for long term, government supported income. Distributions come from rent, not from a sale, which is why we are patient about when we sell.

Why Trident

An edge you can explain.

We buy at a structural discount, not a negotiated one. Our opportunities are priced by process, not by a seller. That is where the margin comes from, and it does not depend on the market going up.

We see more than anyone else in our markets. Comprehensive coverage, refreshed daily, means we are never waiting for a broker to call. Deal flow is the scarcest thing in real estate, and ours is proprietary.

We hold without leverage. Unlevered ownership and government supported rent make for income that does not flinch when credit tightens.

We are operators. The people who built the platform and the people who run the homes are the people you will meet. There is no layer between you and the decision.

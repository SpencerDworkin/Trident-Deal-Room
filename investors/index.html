05

Questions investors ask

Where does the return come from?

From buying below value and holding for income. The discount is captured at acquisition and the income is paid largely by housing authorities. We do not rely on appreciation, leverage or a timed exit.

What does the technology actually do?

It reads public records across our markets every day, identifies residential assets priced well below their value, and ranks them for our principals. It is a sourcing and screening platform. It does not commit capital.

Who decides what to buy?

A principal, every time, against a ceiling set from the underwriting before any commitment is made.

Why no leverage?

Because the strategy does not need it and the income is better without it. Unlevered ownership means no refinancing risk, no lender covenants and no forced sale in a down market.

How is the portfolio operated?

By Patel Legacy Properties and its operating partners, residential owners and operators in the Northeast. Homes are brought to inspection standard and leased for the long term.

What do investors receive?

Quarterly distributions from net rental income, quarterly property level reporting, annual audited financial statements, an investor portal and direct access to both principals.

Can I see the pipeline before committing?

Yes. Qualified investors are shown the platform and the current pipeline in person as part of diligence.

Do you project returns?

Not on this website. Targets and the full economics are set out in the private placement memorandum, with the risk factors beside them.

06

Risks

Private real estate is illiquid and involves risk, including the loss of the entire investment. The principal risks of this strategy include title and surviving encumbrances on acquired properties, the time and cost of obtaining possession, property condition, delays in deployment, dependence on public housing programs, concentration, model and data error, reliance on two principals, and regulatory change. Trident is newly formed and has no operating history. The memorandum sets out the risk factors in full, and so does our disclosures page.

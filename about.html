Our Story

A Legacy Built on Real Estate

LLD Enterprises is a Rochester, New York-based family-owned real estate investment and development firm. We combine family capital with deep expertise in acquiring, developing, and managing high-quality commercial real estate assets.

Our roots trace to Sheldon A. Lane, an engineer who co-founded Monro Muffler Brake of Rochester — the largest company-owned muffler and brake chain in the United States. When Monro was sold in 1984 and later taken public, Lane and partners retained all company real estate holdings across multiple states, which became the foundation of our diverse national portfolio.

After Sheldon Lane's passing in 1994, his daughter Wendy and son-in-law David Dworkin formed LLD Enterprises and assumed leadership, continuing the family's commitment to excellence in commercial real estate.

Partnership

LLD Enterprises & JADD Management

A pivotal moment came when David Dworkin met John August — son of Burton August Sr., a Monro co-founder — during a competitive bid. Rather than compete, they partnered to acquire a Rochester office complex, establishing JADD Management.

Today, LLD Enterprises and JADD Management jointly operate as a fully integrated real estate platform, combining decades of experience with a vertically integrated team that guides projects from site selection through construction and opening.

We are hands-on, proactive owners and managers who pay strict attention to detail and deliver professional, responsive service to our tenants and partners.

Leadership

Executive Team

Experienced professionals with decades of expertise in commercial real estate.

DD

David Dworkin

Co-Founder, Managing Partner, Licensed Real Estate Broker

David manages asset operations, acquisition, and development functions. He oversees portfolio leasing, directs financial operations, leads investment strategy, and sources real estate opportunities while managing underwriting and transaction closure.

David holds a Bachelor of Arts from Syracuse University and a Master of Science in Real Estate from NYU. He was named New York State Commercial Realtor of the Year in 2003 and received the NYSCAR Lifetime Achievement Award in 2009 as the youngest recipient ever.

He is a Charter member and past State President of the New York State Commercial Association of Realtors, was appointed to the New York State Board of Real Estate in 2006, and serves on numerous community boards including Champion Academy and Allendale Columbia School.

In 2013, David and Wendy co-purchased the NBA's Sacramento Kings. In 2015, they purchased the Rochester Rhinos soccer club, and in 2026 acquired Kelty Hearts Football Club in Scotland, where David serves as Vice-Chairman.

JA

John August

Co-Founder, Partner, Licensed Associate Real Estate Broker

John oversees property management, construction, and approvals with over 30 years in real estate. He started at Monro Muffler Brake Inc. and was promoted in 1983 to lead Rochester expansion efforts.

He oversaw site acquisition, approvals, and construction, directing nearly 300 store openings across 15 new states over the next decade. Promoted in 1988 to Senior V.P., adding Facilities, Maintenance, and Training oversight. After retiring in 1998 following 26 years of service, he founded J. August Properties Inc.

John partnered with David Dworkin in 2000 to establish JADD Management. He currently serves as President of Hillel School in Brighton, NY.

WD

Wendy Dworkin, Esq.

Partner, Licensed Associate Real Estate Broker

Wendy handles lease negotiation and drafting for the portfolio and manages contractual arrangements with outside legal counsel. She holds a Bachelor of Arts from Syracuse University and a Juris Doctorate from Benjamin Cardozo School of Law, Yeshiva University.

A licensed New York State Real Estate salesperson/broker since 1989, Wendy is a member of the Monroe County and New York State Bar Associations and a New York State Part 36 Receiver.

In 2016, she was inducted into the Rochester Jewish Sports Hall of Fame. Together with David, she received the Syracuse University Most Outstanding Young Alumni of the Year award in 2006 and the Syracuse University Alumni Club Community Service Award in 2019.

JS

Jeffrey Saeger

Partner, Licensed Associate Real Estate Broker

Jeff handles development, construction, and operations. His experience spans 17 years in entrepreneurial real estate ventures, including being Microtel Franchise and Development's first employee, managing a family hotel enterprise for 11 years, and founding JAS Properties LLC for multi-family apartments.

Jeff joined JADD Management in 2004 and holds a Bachelor of Science from the Cornell University School of Hotel Administration.

MS

Michael Smith

Director of Leasing, Licensed Real Estate Salesperson

Michael oversees the leasing process, brokerage services, tenant relations, and marketing with over 26 years in commercial real estate. He began his career with Cushman Wakefield/Pyramid Brokerage and CBRE, spending 11 years in brokerage.

He then moved to development with DDR, serving as Leasing Director for Northeast shopping centers (5-7 million square feet). He worked with Fairmount Properties on the $100M mixed-use College Town development at the University of Rochester, and most recently held the Vice President of Leasing position at The DiMarco Group.

Michael holds an A.A.S. in Retail Business Management and a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration/Marketing from Rochester Institute of Technology. He is an active ICSC member and has served as Board President for Saints and Sinners, Inc.

SS

Shane Seaburg, CPA

Controller

Shane oversees 1.5 million square feet of commercial real estate across 12 shopping centers in 3 states in a joint venture with a publicly traded Canadian REIT. He manages accounting, budgeting, financial reporting, debt covenant compliance, tenant accounts, and collections.

With 14 years of accounting experience across public and private organizations, Shane previously served as Senior Financial Analyst in FP&A at Carestream Health, Inc. and as Controller at Waterleau Inc.

He holds a Bachelor of Science from SUNY Brockport, completed an Accounting program at St. John Fisher College, and is a Certified Public Accountant. He also serves as Member and Treasurer of Capital on the Edge, Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit supporting at-risk youth in Nicaragua.

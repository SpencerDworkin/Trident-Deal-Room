What We Do

A fully integrated real estate platform delivering comprehensive solutions across every stage of the investment lifecycle.

⚗

Acquisitions

Unlike many other real estate investment firms, we are deal-specific, cap-rate buyers based on the market cycle. We pursue core-plus, value-add, and opportunistic investments across all asset types, focusing on property value enhancement through asset management, leasing, and redevelopment.

Our acquisition activities are concentrated in eastern, southeastern, and midwestern U.S. locations.

Retail

Community, neighborhood, and power multi-tenant open air centers; single-tenant opportunistic investments nationwide.

Office / Medical Office

Core-plus, value-add, and opportunistic opportunities in Upstate New York.

Industrial / Flex

Multi-tenant and single-tenant assets in the eastern United States.

Multifamily

A, B, and C quality apartments — mid-rise, high-rise, and garden style in Western New York.

Sale / Leaseback

Facilitating transactions throughout the United States.

⛪

Development

We have built a reputation as a leading single-tenant and multi-tenant development firm with proven expertise in site selection, acquisition, entitlement, zoning, infrastructure, construction, and project management.

Our vertically integrated team guides projects from site selection through approvals, construction, and opening. We focus on building lasting partnerships and community relationships while unlocking portfolio value through strategic development.

✍

Brokerage Services

We offer complete commercial real estate brokerage solutions for buying, selling, or leasing. Our team provides landlord and tenant representation in commercial, industrial, and investment transactions.

Our brokerage professionals manage our company portfolio leasing and marketing, providing them with invaluable hands-on market experience that benefits every client engagement.

Tenant Representation Services

  • Strategic analysis for store development
  • Market analysis with demographic focus
  • Site selection and evaluation
  • Lease negotiation
  • Competition mapping and analysis
  • Construction assistance
  • Post-lease coordination

We serve national, regional, and local retailers seeking expansion in new and existing markets.

Asset & Property Management

As owners of commercial properties, our tenants are our clients. Our goal is to operate our portfolio and the properties we manage efficiently, economically, and safely. We provide third-party management and court-appointed receiver (Part 36) services for quality office, retail, and industrial properties.

Asset Management

  • Cash basis and/or accrual accounting
  • Valuation and underwriting advisory
  • Financial reporting
  • Loan servicing
  • Cash management and collections/disbursements
  • Accounts payable and receivables management
  • Partner distributions
  • Annual budgeting and expense management
  • Quarterly reporting with budget comparisons
  • Additional rent reconciliations
  • Asset performance assessment
  • Tax reporting interface

Property Management

  • Property maintenance and facilities management
  • Construction management
  • Operating and capital expense implementation
  • Third-party vendor management
  • Construction services
  • Risk management and insurance advisory
  • Governmental regulations coordination
  • Tenant approvals and advisory

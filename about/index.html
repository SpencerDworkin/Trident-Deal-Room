About

A small firm, run by its owners.

Trident is a Patel Legacy Properties company, formed in 2026 to buy commercial property in New Jersey and the surrounding states with a small group of private investors. There are two principals, and they are the people you will deal with.

Principals

Who we are

SD

Spencer Dworkin

Principal

Spencer built the software Trident uses to find property and leads sourcing, data and underwriting. He studied real estate at New York University’s Schack Institute of Real Estate, where he served as co-portfolio manager of the student run NYU Schack REIT Investment Fund, a public real estate portfolio that the team concentrated from roughly twenty positions into ten high conviction names during his tenure. AP

Ayush Patel

Principal · Patel Legacy Properties

Ayush is a commercial real estate broker and investor who has spent his career in Northeast retail. At NAI James E. Hanson in Teterboro, New Jersey, he specializes in shopping centers, retail pad sites, mixed use buildings and highest and best use redevelopment, and he is licensed in New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania. Before that he worked for a Bergen County developer of grocery anchored shopping centers, where he supported the refinancing of three centers and worked alongside JLL on a $110 million sale of five grocery anchored properties. He founded Patel Legacy Properties in 2024. He holds an M.S. in Real Estate from NYU’s Schack Institute, where he served on the SPS Dean’s Advisory Council, and a B.S. in real estate finance from Rutgers Business School. He is a member of ULI and ICSC.

Patel Legacy Properties

The parent company

Patel Legacy Properties is the real estate investment firm Ayush Patel founded in 2024, focused on the acquisition and disposition of retail and mixed use property in the Northeast and on working with private and institutional capital. Its market knowledge and relationships, built over years in Northeast retail, are the foundation Trident is built on. Trident is a separate company with its own technology and its own deals.

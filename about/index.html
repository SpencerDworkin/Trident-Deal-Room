How we work

Technology sources. People decide. Our platform reads the whole market and ranks what it finds. A principal underwrites every opportunity and sets the price in advance. The platform never commits capital.

Discipline over volume. We would rather pass on ten homes than overpay for one. A ceiling set before a commitment is a ceiling, and it does not move.

Transparency with investors. Our investors see the portfolio property by property, every quarter, and they can reach either principal directly.

Alignment. The principals invest in the fund on the same terms as investors.

03

Patel Legacy Properties

Patel Legacy Properties is a residential real estate operator in the Northeast. Its experience in acquiring, renovating and leasing homes, and in working with the housing authorities that support them, is the operating foundation of Trident. The technology is Trident’s own. The fund is a separate vehicle with its own capital.

Trident is newly formed. It does not yet have an operating history or assets under management, and we say so plainly. What it has is a platform that is already running, an operating partner that already operates, and a strategy that can be explained in a sentence.

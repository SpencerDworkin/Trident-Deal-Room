Strategy

Buy below value. Hold for income.

Trident pursues a single strategy and executes it with discipline: acquire residential real estate at a structural discount to its value, pay cash, and hold it for long term income supported by public housing programs. Our proprietary platform finds the opportunities. Our principals decide which ones to pursue.

01

The opportunity

Every year a meaningful share of residential real estate in the Northeast changes hands through processes where the price is set by circumstance rather than by a willing seller. These assets rarely reach the open market. They are scattered across public records, they move on tight timelines, and they are too numerous for any team of people to follow comprehensively.

That is the gap Trident was built to close. Our platform reads the whole market continuously, so that nothing of interest escapes our attention and nothing of interest is bought in haste.

02

The discount

We do not negotiate our discount. We find it. The assets we pursue are priced by a process that has nothing to do with what the home is worth, which is precisely why value can sit so far from price. Our platform measures that gap for every asset in our markets and ranks them.

Because the margin is built in at acquisition, our returns do not depend on appreciation, on refinancing, or on a market that keeps rising. They depend on paying the right price, which is the one thing we control completely.

03

The asset

We buy modest homes of one to four units in established neighborhoods. They are the most liquid form of real estate in America, the easiest to lease, and the easiest to sell. They attract the deepest pool of tenants and the deepest pool of buyers, which protects both our income and our exit.

We avoid what we cannot underwrite with confidence. Complexity is not a source of return for us. Price is.

04

The income

Once stabilized, our homes are leased for the long term under tenancies supported by public housing programs, in which the greater part of the rent is paid directly by a housing authority on a published schedule. The result is income that is steady, inspected, and largely insulated from the credit of any single tenant.

We hold without leverage. There is no lender to satisfy, no maturity to refinance, and no reason to sell into a bad market. Distributions come from rent.

05

The discipline

Our platform proposes. It never commits capital. Every opportunity it surfaces is underwritten by a principal, who confirms title, condition, occupancy and value before setting a ceiling. The ceiling is set in advance and it is not raised. We would rather lose a home than overpay for it, and we do, regularly.

The full methodology is proprietary. We walk qualified investors through it in person.

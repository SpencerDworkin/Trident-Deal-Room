The principals

Two principals, one book, every sale attended.

Trident is a subsidiary of Pursuit Lending Partners. It is run by its two principals, who screen every filing, read every title, set every ceiling and stand at every sale themselves. Investors report to the same book the principals work from each morning.

How we operate

Small by design.

The screen is code. The buy box is written as rules with numbers in them and run on every filing in every covered county. Neither principal can override a verdict for a property they like; the rules change for everyone or not at all.

Every file carries the checklist. Ten items, nine of them blocking, each with a date that hangs off the sale. A file with a blocking item open on sale day is not bid on.

A principal attends every sale. Ceilings are set from the underwriting three days out and written down. The person in the room is the person who set the number, and the number does not move.

Investors see the working book. Reporting is not a summary prepared for investors; it is the book itself, quarterly, property by property. There is nothing to reconcile because there is only one document.

Pursuit Lending Partners. Trident is a subsidiary of PLP and draws on its lending and real-estate underwriting background. The sheriff’s-sale program is Trident’s own, with its own vehicle and its own capital.

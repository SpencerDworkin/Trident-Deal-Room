Markets

Buying in New Jersey. Raising along the corridor.

Trident buys where the foreclosure process is judicial, public and calendared, and where the housing authority pays enough to carry a modest home. It raises from family offices within a few hours’ drive of the properties, and in Florida, where many of them winter.

1

Where we buy

Each county carries two numbers that matter: the median value, which is the denominator of the screen, and the three-bedroom Fair Market Rent, which is what the housing authority will pay and so what the property can yield. A county whose FMR cannot clear 5% on a reasonable basis is excluded from the voucher strategy, however good its discounts look. Two counties are not yet live because their sheriffs publish differently; both are being brought on.

Why New Jersey

Judicial foreclosure. Every sale follows a court judgment, so the debt figure the screen runs on is a court record rather than a lender’s estimate, and sales are calendared weeks ahead by county sheriffs. That is what makes a disciplined pre-bid process possible.

The housing stock. The covered counties are dense with exactly the modest detached and small-multi homes that voucher holders look for and housing authorities approve readily.

Fair Market Rents that carry a modest basis. In most covered counties the three-bedroom FMR supports a 5% unlevered yield on the basis the screen produces. Where it does not, the county is out, and the table says so.

Beginning here, not ending here. Any judicial-foreclosure state with public sale calendars and a functioning voucher program is a candidate, and the screen is pointed at a new county by loading its median and its FMR. New Jersey is where the book is; the ambition is national.

Where we raise

Family offices in New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut, because an allocator who can drive to a property in an afternoon is an allocator who understands what they own; and Florida, because a good many of the same families are there for part of the year.

The offering is private, to accredited investors and qualified purchasers, with a minimum commitment of $1,000,000. We are not raising from the public, and we would rather have a small number of investors who have walked a property than a large number who have read a deck.

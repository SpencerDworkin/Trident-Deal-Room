Markets

Buying in New Jersey. Raising along the corridor.

Trident buys where the foreclosure process is judicial, public and calendared, and where the housing authority pays enough to carry a modest home. It raises from family offices within a few hours’ drive of the properties, plus Florida, where many of them winter.

Where we buy

Six counties live. Two more coming.

Each county is modelled on its own median value and its own Fair Market Rent against the 5% unlevered hurdle.

Every county carries two numbers that matter: the median value, which is the denominator of the screen, and the three-bedroom Fair Market Rent, which is what the housing authority will pay and therefore what the property can yield. A county whose FMR cannot clear 5% on a reasonable basis is excluded from the voucher strategy, however good its discounts look.

Coverage means the sheriff’s calendar is being pulled and screened. Two counties are not yet live because their sheriffs publish differently; both are being brought on.

Why New Jersey

The process is public, and the stock is right.

Judicial foreclosure. Every sale follows a court judgment, so the debt figure the screen runs on is a court record rather than a lender’s estimate. Sales are calendared weeks ahead by county sheriffs, which is what makes a disciplined pre-bid process possible at all.

One-to-four unit housing stock. The counties Trident covers are dense with exactly the modest, detached and small-multi homes that voucher holders are looking for and that housing authorities inspect and approve readily.

Fair Market Rents that carry a modest basis. In most of the covered counties the three-bedroom FMR supports a 5% unlevered yield on the basis the screen produces. Where it does not, the county is out, and the table says so.

Beginning here, not ending here. The method is not specific to New Jersey. Any judicial-foreclosure state with public sale calendars and a functioning voucher program is a candidate, and the screen is built to be pointed at a new county by loading its median and its FMR. New Jersey is where the book is; the ambition is national.

Where we raise

Family offices, within reach of the properties.

Trident is raising preferred equity from family offices in New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Connecticut and Florida — the first four because an allocator who can drive to a property in an afternoon is an allocator who understands what they own, and Florida because a good many of them are there for part of the year.

The offering is private, to accredited investors and qualified purchasers, with a minimum of $1,000,000. We are not raising from the public, and we would rather have a small number of investors who have walked a property than a large number who have read a deck.

Preferred Equity

How our partners invest.

Trident National raises preferred equity for each property it buys, from a small group of partners in a single entity per deal. Partners are paid first, from the property’s income, before we earn anything on our own interest. The structure is simple and it is the same every time.

01

The structure

Each property is bought and held in its own limited liability company. Our partners hold preferred equity in that company. Trident National invests its own capital alongside theirs and holds the common interest, which is paid only after the preferred return has been paid.

Preferred means first in line for the property’s income. It is a priority of payment, not a guarantee. It is paid when the property earns it, and the offering documents for each property set out exactly how it accrues and how it is paid.

02

Terms

Minimum investment
$250,000 per partner.
Partners per property
Up to four partners in one entity, alongside Trident National.
Preferred return, $250,000 to $1,000,000

Distributions
Quarterly, from the property’s net income.
Reporting
Quarterly, property by property, and a principal on the phone when you want one.
Hold
We buy to hold for income. A sale is decided property by property, with the partners in that property.
Who may invest
Accredited investors. Most of our partners are people we know or have been introduced to.

These are the terms on which we expect to offer preferred equity in each property. The offering documents for a specific property control, and may differ. A preferred return is paid from the income of one property and depends on that property performing. It is not a projection of any result and nothing here is a guarantee.

03

Why preferred equity

You are paid first. The preferred return comes out of the property’s income before Trident National earns anything on its own interest. Our capital sits behind yours.

One property, one entity. You know exactly what you own, what it earns and who the tenants are. There is no pool and no blind commitment.

Income, not a promise of a sale. Distributions come from rent. We do not need a buyer, a refinancing or a rising market to pay you.

Discipline you can see. Every property is bought below value through a court supervised sale or an off market purchase, at a price written down before we commit. The basis is what protects your preferred position.

04

How it works

1. Talk to us
A conversation with a principal about what we are buying and whether it suits you.
2. The property
When we have a property under contract or a sale date, you receive the underwriting, the records and the offering documents for that entity.
3. Commit
Subscription documents and accredited investor confirmation. Capital is funded at closing, not before.
4. Own
Quarterly distributions and reporting from the day the property produces income.

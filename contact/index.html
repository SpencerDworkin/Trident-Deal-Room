What happens next

Three steps, no sales process.

We would rather have a few investors who have read the book and walked a property than many who have seen a deck. The sequence is built for that.

01

The memorandum and the book

Within a day you receive the confidential private placement memorandum and the current screened book — every filing in the covered counties, how the screen sorted it, and the files being worked.

02

A call with both principals

Not a presentation. Bring the questions the materials raised. We will walk through specific files on the calendar and show how the ceiling on each was set.

03

Subscription, if it fits

Accredited-investor or qualified-purchaser verification, subscription documents, and funding into the vehicle’s account. Capital is deployed as files clear the screen and the checklist.

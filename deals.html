How the desk works

From docket to deed

Filings hit the desk the morning they publish. Everything you see above has already been through valuation, scope, and financing before it reaches you.

Docket ingest

Sheriff sale notices, tax foreclosure lists, referee filings, and REO dispositions across six counties are pulled and normalized to one record format.

Valuation & scope

Each parcel is valued against paired comps and given a condition read and a rehab budget, so an opening bid can be compared to a basis, not a list price.

Underwrite & score

Flip, buy-and-hold, and BRRRR are all modeled — NOI, DSCR, cash-on-cash, maximum allowable offer — and blended into one composite score.

Bid & take title

We hand you the bidder checklist, deposit terms, and lien position, then stand behind you at the courthouse steps and through the referee's deed.

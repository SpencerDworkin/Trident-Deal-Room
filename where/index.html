The Opportunity

Why now.

Four forces are converging on the assets we buy: a rising supply of distressed homes, prices set well below value, the deepest housing shortage in the country, and a federal law that removes the largest buyers from the market in January 2027.

01

Supply is rising

Foreclosure filings in the United States rose 14% in 2025 to 367,460 properties, and completed foreclosure auctions in the third quarter of 2026 were 25% higher than a year earlier, the highest quarterly total since the first quarter of 2020. It was the seventh consecutive quarter of growth. Scheduled auction volume for the final quarter of 2026 is running well ahead of last year.

New Jersey ranked sixth among states by foreclosure rate in 2025, with one in every 273 housing units receiving a filing, and had the highest rate of any state in December. This is a judicial foreclosure state with long timelines, which means the pipeline visible today is the supply of the next two years.

Sources: ATTOM Year End 2025 U.S. Foreclosure Market Report; Auction.com Auction Market Dispatch, Q3 2026.

02

Price is set below value

At a foreclosure auction the opening bid is set by the lender’s claim, not by what the home is worth. In the second quarter of 2026, buyers at foreclosure auctions nationally paid an average of 66.5% of estimated retail market value. The discount is structural. It exists because the process is fast, public, cash only and unforgiving, and because the number of buyers equipped to underwrite it properly is small.

That is the margin our strategy is built on. It is captured on the day we buy.

Source: Auction.com Auction Market Dispatch, Q2 2026.

03

The Northeast builds the least

The United States is short an estimated 3.7 to 4 million homes. The shortage is most acute at the entry level, and nowhere more so than in the Northeast. Connecticut, New Jersey and Rhode Island are the only states to have lost housing units per capita since 2020. New Jersey added roughly 260,000 residents in that period and about 66,500 homes, and needs 350,000 to 400,000 new homes over the next decade.

A modest home in an established Northeast neighborhood is a scarce asset with the deepest pool of tenants and buyers in the country. Buying one below value is a durable position.

Sources: Freddie Mac; Zillow; Stateline, May 2026; New Jersey Housing Governing Council.

04

The largest buyers are leaving

The 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act became federal law on July 11, 2026. From January 7, 2027 it bars any investor that controls 350 or more single family homes from purchasing homes of one or two units, including through foreclosure. The institutions that defined this market for a decade, and that paid the most for it, are now out of it for existing homes.

Trident operates far below that threshold. We expect to buy, for years, into a market where our largest competitors are prohibited from bidding.

Sources: Mayer Brown, Latham & Watkins and Goodwin client alerts on the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, July 2026.

05

The rent is public money

Housing authorities paid rent on behalf of more than two million households last year through the Housing Choice Voucher program, funded at $38.4 billion for fiscal 2026. The authority pays its share directly to the owner on a published schedule, and demand for the homes that qualify exceeds supply in every market we cover. Waiting lists run for years.

Experienced affordable housing investors describe this income as the most stable in residential real estate, with negligible default on the authority’s share and occupancy that holds in a downturn. It is the income our strategy is built to collect.

Sources: U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development; National Low Income Housing Coalition; Avanath Capital Management and Nuveen public commentary.

Where We Invest

Where the platform finds mispricing.

We invest in markets that meet our criteria: a judicial foreclosure process with a transparent public record, deep rental demand, constrained new supply, and housing authority payment standards that support a modest home. New Jersey, our home market, meets every one, and our platform is built to extend to any market that does. Our investors come from anywhere.

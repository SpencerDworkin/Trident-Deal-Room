Markets

The Northeast corridor.

We invest where demand is deep, supply is constrained and public support for housing is strong. The corridor from New York through New Jersey, Pennsylvania and Connecticut is the most durable rental market in the country, and it is where our principals have operated for years.

01

Why the Northeast

The Northeast builds less housing than it needs and has done so for decades. Its towns are established, its neighborhoods are stable, and its rental demand is the deepest in America. The same conditions that make it hard to build here make what already exists valuable, and make a home bought below value a durable asset.

Public support for housing is also at its strongest here. Housing authorities across the corridor pay reliable, published rents for modest homes, which is the income our strategy is built on.

02

New Jersey first

New Jersey is our home market and the first market the platform covers in full. It is the most densely populated state in the country, sits between two of its largest cities, and has a large and consistent flow of the opportunities we pursue. Our principals know its towns, its courthouses and its housing authorities firsthand.

We are active across the state's most populous counties and expanding our coverage steadily.

03

Extending across the corridor

The platform was designed to add markets without rebuilding. As Trident Fund I deploys, we expect to extend into neighboring markets in New York, Pennsylvania and Connecticut where the same fundamentals hold. Each market is added only when our coverage of it is complete and our principals can underwrite there with the same confidence they have at home.

Where We Raise

Family offices and private investors.

Trident Fund I is offered to accredited investors and qualified purchasers in New Jersey, New York, Pennsylvania, Connecticut and Florida, by invitation.

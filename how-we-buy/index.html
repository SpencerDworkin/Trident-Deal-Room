Technology

A platform that reads the whole market.

Real estate has always rewarded whoever sees the opportunity first. Trident built a proprietary platform so that we see all of them. It gathers public records across our markets every day, identifies the assets where price and value have come apart, and ranks them for our principals to underwrite.

The Platform

From data to ownership

Two stages are software. Three are people. The line between them is deliberate.

01

Comprehensive coverage

The opportunities we pursue are recorded in public, but they are recorded in dozens of places, in inconsistent forms, on timelines that change without notice. Following them by hand means following a fraction of them.

Our platform follows all of them. It gathers the records continuously, reconciles them into a single view of every asset in our markets, and refreshes that view every day. When a date moves or a new opportunity appears, we know the same day.

02

Proprietary intelligence

Coverage alone is noise. The value of the platform is in what it does with the records: measuring, for every asset, how far the price on offer sits from what the home is worth, and setting aside the many cases where an apparent discount is not real.

The models apply the same written standards to every asset, every time. Nothing is favored because someone likes it. The output is a ranked set of opportunities with the reasoning attached, ready for a principal to open.

03

Human judgment

No model commits our capital. A principal underwrites each opportunity the platform surfaces: title, condition, occupancy, value and exit. From that work a ceiling is set, in writing, before any commitment is made. If the underwriting does not support the price, we pass.

This is the division of labor we believe in. Software is better than people at reading everything. People are better than software at deciding what to own.

04

Built to extend

The platform was built for New Jersey and designed for the Northeast. Adding a market is a matter of connecting its records, not rebuilding the system. As the fund grows, so does the ground the platform covers.

Our data sources, models and criteria are proprietary. We present the platform to qualified investors in person and describe it in detail in the private placement memorandum.

In Short

The platform proposes. People commit capital.

That sentence governs everything we do. It is why our deal flow is proprietary and why our discipline never depends on a good day in the room.
